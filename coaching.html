Perfect. I’ll teach you everything you need to know. We start with the basics and build from there. You don’t need to be an expert, you just need to be willing to learn.

What if I can’t work out 5 days a week?

You don’t have to! I’ll meet you where you are. Whether you’re working out 2 days a week or 5, we’ll build a plan that fits your life, your recovery capacity, and your goals.

Honestly? That depends on you. I can give you the best plan in the world, but you have to do the work. What I CAN promise is support, accountability, and adjustments when life gets messy. This isn’t about perfection, it’s about consistency and progress.

What if I want to cancel?

This is month-to-month, so you can cancel anytime with 30 days’ notice. That said, I recommend committing to at least 3 months to see real, lasting results.

Do you accept insurance?

No, this is a private pay coaching service and is not covered by insurance.

What if we’re not a good fit?

That’s what the discovery call is for. If I don’t think I can help you, I’ll tell you honestly and point you toward resources that might be a better fit.
