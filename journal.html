Whether you’re just starting, adjusting to a new dose, or weaning off, GLP-1 Journal helps you build the habits and identity that make your results last.

If you’ve ever started a program strong, only to lose focus a few weeks in, this journal is your antidote. Designed for GLP-1 users, it gives you a few minutes of prompts each day and a new theme each week, so you stay consistent, connected, and in control through your first year.

What’s Inside:

  • Daily Entries: Quick prompts to check in on hunger, movement, and mindset in under 5 minutes
  • Weekly Themes: A year of themes, from riding the urge to rebuilding food trust, each with its own affirmation
  • Weekly Reflections: Look back on your wins, notice cravings and how you responded, and set an intention for the week ahead
  • Emotional Regulation Prompts: CBT/DBT-inspired questions to help you ride out cravings, shift thought patterns, and reset after tough days
  • Progress Selfies: Add a photo to any reflection and watch your journey play back as a slideshow
  • Your Streak at a Glance: A calendar of the days you showed up, plus an optional daily reminder

Why It Works:

GLP-1s calm your hunger, and this journal helps you listen to everything else.

It keeps your habits front and center, your mindset sharp, and your journey personal. Skip the overwhelm and the mental chaos, this is a place where you can show up daily and stay grounded in the person you’re becoming.

And it’s yours alone. There’s no account, no ads, and no tracking. Your entries stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which devices does it work on?

GLP-1 Journal is for iPhone, running iOS 17 or later. If iCloud is on, your journal syncs to your other iPhones signed in to the same Apple ID.

What does it cost?

Your first 7 entries are free. After that, Premium is $1.99 a month or $19.99 a year for unlimited entries. Subscriptions are handled by Apple, and you can cancel any time in your App Store settings.

Who can see my journal?

Only you. There’s no account to create and no analytics or tracking. Your entries and photos are stored on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud, which we can’t access.

Can I export or delete my entries?

Yes. You can export your journal as a spreadsheet (CSV) from Settings in the app. To delete everything, see the steps in our Privacy Policy.

Is this medical advice?

No. GLP-1 Journal is a journaling tool, not a substitute for care from your doctor or prescriber. Talk to them about your medication, dosing, and health.
